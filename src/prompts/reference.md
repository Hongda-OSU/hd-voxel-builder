Reference images are attached. Match them; this overrides the preset where they conflict.
- Match the overall silhouette and proportions of the image, not the preset: width against
  height, how big the head is, whether legs show. Never add what the image does not show
  (no legs under a cape or long coat that hides them).
- Build the big masses first at the full width and length they have in the image: hair
  volume, long locks that hang past the face, capes that widen towards the ground, collars
  and shoulder pieces. Then add the details on top.
- Rebuild every distinct shape you can see: the hair silhouette (spikes, side locks, parting),
  collars, layers of clothing, capes, accessories. Do not stop at 3 features.
- Use up to 16 colours. Give each material one flat colour, but keep colour regions the
  image clearly separates (for example lit hair and shadowed hair ends) as separate colours.
  Do not copy per-pixel highlights or dithering.
- Use mirrorX only where the reference is symmetric; keep asymmetric hair and poses.
- Read the image as the front view (x across, h up) and give every shape a believable depth.
- Spend the parts budget: use as many parts and cells as the details need.
