Style preset: diorama, a small scene on a base. Default size [32, 32, 24].
- h = 0 to 1 is a base plate over the whole footprint: grass, sand, floor or road.
  Chamfer it with "v" only; "all" would remove a box this thin.
- Everything else stands on the base and connects to it; nothing overhangs its edge.
- At most 4 main objects. Put the tallest at the back and small ones at the front, so the
  default view shows all of them, and leave a voxel or two of base around them.
- People in a scene keep chibi proportions at a smaller scale, about 10 to 14 tall.
- Put moving parts (a door, a flag, a person) in their own group.
