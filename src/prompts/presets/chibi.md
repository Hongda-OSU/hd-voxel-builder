Style preset: chibi character. Default size [26, 16, 36].
- Head plus hair is about half the total height; the body is about half the head width.
- Legs are 3 to 5 voxels tall; the feet stand on h = 0.
- Hair is a shell one voxel larger than the head; the fringe sticks out one voxel at the
  front and covers the top two rows of the forehead; back hair reaches the bottom of the shell.
- Eyes are 2 wide and 3 tall on the front face, painted with paint: true: the top two rows
  dark, the bottom row dark blue, and one white highlight in the middle row in the right
  column of both eyes. Write both eyes as boxes in one part instead of using mirrorX, so the
  highlights stay on the same side.
- Put moving parts (an arm that dribbles, a held ball) in their own group.
